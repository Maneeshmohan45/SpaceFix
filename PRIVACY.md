# Privacy Policy for SpaceFix

**Effective Date:** October 27, 2026

SpaceFix ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data.

---

### 1. Zero Data Collection
SpaceFix is an offline, client-side browser extension. **We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data, keystrokes, browsing history, or user information.**

### 2. Local Processing Only
- All spell-checking algorithms and dictionary lookups execute 100% locally within your browser tab's isolated runtime.
- Keystrokes are processed solely in memory on your device to detect spacebar typos and perform atomic undos. Keystroke data is never recorded, logged, or transmitted anywhere.

### 3. Permissions Used
- **`storage`**: Used exclusively to store your personal extension settings locally on your machine (such as whether auto-correct is toggled on/off, your custom website whitelist, and local correction counters). This data never leaves your device.

### 4. Remote Code & Network Activity
- SpaceFix does not use remote code, external scripts, analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party advertising SDKs.
- The extension performs **zero network requests**.

### 5. Contact & Open Source
SpaceFix is an open-source project. You can inspect the complete source code at:
[https://github.com/Maneeshmohan45/SpaceFix](https://github.com/Maneeshmohan45/SpaceFix)

If you have any questions, you can open an issue on our GitHub repository.
